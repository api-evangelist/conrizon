Essen/Straubing, June 16, 2026 – conrizon AG continues to grow: Through its subsidiary easy software Deutschland GmbH, the group has acquired HENRICHSEN4easy GmbH, based in Straubing. With this move, the European ECM holding company is strategically expanding its brand portfolio to include additional document management solutions as well as specialized industry offerings—particularly for the utilities and waste management sectors and the public service sector.
